Integrations

Where findings enter, and where the fix is approved

Slack and Microsoft Teams are available today. Both Marketplace listings are in review. Scanner files listed below are the formats accepted now.

Slack

Marketplace: in review

Sign up with Slack and run remediation from cards in the workspace: assign a finding, approve a step, and close it with the audit record attached. The Slack app is available for that workflow.

The Slack Marketplace listing is in review.

Microsoft Teams

Marketplace: in review

Sign up with Microsoft Teams and use the same remediation cards inside Teams: the plan, the approval, and the closure stay on the finding.

The Microsoft Teams Marketplace listing is in review.

Scanner and assessment formats accepted today

  • Nessus files
  • AWS Security Hub exports
  • Custom assessment files

Configuration and CIS benchmark results are taken in when they are already inside one of those uploaded reports. There is no separate live connector for other scanners.
