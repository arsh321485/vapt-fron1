Security and trust

What happens to a finding after you upload it

This page states what is sent to the model, what is kept out of logs, where data is hosted, how it is protected, how long it is kept, who the subprocessors are, and where to report a vulnerability.

What is sent to the model

OpenAI, L.L.C. is the model provider. It is a subprocessor because customer vulnerability findings from uploaded assessment reports are sent to it. The model uses that material to extract and classify findings and to write remediation recommendations: steps, a script where automation applies, verification, and rollback.

Slack data and customer vulnerability data are not used to train or fine-tune VAPTFIX's own models, or third-party models. Customer reports are not used to train VAPTFIX's own AI models.

What is masked

Sensitive credentials, OAuth tokens, and secrets are redacted from operational logs. Slack OAuth tokens are encrypted and are not written to application logs or returned in API responses. The finding text needed to build the remediation plan is what is sent to the model. Passwords and tokens are not part of that plan.

Hosting

Application data is hosted with Contabo GmbH in France. Account, assessment, and operational data in the database are on MongoDB Atlas in Frankfurt (eu-central-1). A customer can request a specific hosting region where that region is supported and written into the customer agreement.

Encryption

  • Connections use TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Databases, file storage, and backups are encrypted with AES-256, or an equivalent control on that infrastructure.
  • Slack OAuth tokens are encrypted at field level with a managed key service.
  • Keys and secrets are rotated at least annually, and immediately if exposure is suspected.

Access control

  • Customer records stay on that customer's account. A user sees only what their role allows.
  • Production data is limited to authorised VAPTFIX personnel with a support or operational need. Support access needs a ticket and stays inside that ticket's scope and time.
  • Privileged and production access uses single sign-on and multi-factor authentication where that workflow applies. This is VAPTFIX staff access, not customer SSO.
  • Access is reviewed quarterly and removed within 24 hours of a role change or departure, where that applies.

Retention

Data is kept only as long as the service, the contract, security, and the law require. The working schedule is below. A written contract or a legal duty can set a different period. Full terms are in the Privacy Policy.

DataPeriod
Slack OAuth tokensDeleted immediately after uninstall or revocation, once that event is processed.
Slack workspace, user, and channel identifiersDeleted within 30 days after uninstall.
Vulnerability findings and remediation recordsDeleted within 30 days after the contract ends, or sooner on an approved request.
Audit logs12 months from the event, unless the contract says otherwise.
Operational logsRolling 30 days.
Encrypted backupsUp to 35 days, then overwritten.

Subprocessors

The live list, including what each provider processes and where, is on the Sub-processors page. Because uploaded vulnerability findings are sent to the model, the model provider is on that list:

  • OpenAI, L.L.C. — vulnerability findings from uploaded assessment reports
  • Contabo GmbH — platform hosting
  • MongoDB Inc. (MongoDB Atlas) — account and assessment data
  • Twilio Inc. (SendGrid) — email
  • Stripe, Inc. — billing

Responsible disclosure

Report a security vulnerability or a security incident to security@vaptfix.ai. Do not include passwords, OAuth tokens, or other secrets in the email.

Privacy requests, including access, correction, export, and deletion, go to privacy@vaptfix.ai.
